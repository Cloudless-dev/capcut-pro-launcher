CAPCUT LAUNCHER — WINDOWS

EXE installer: download and install the launcher.
ZIP: extract the archive, then open CapCut Launcher.exe.
Install the official CapCut app first.

Add a profile or choose your current CapCut account.
Automatic sign-in on: enter email and password in the launcher.
Automatic sign-in off: sign in through the website window.
Choose a region and click Launch CapCut.
Close CapCut before switching profiles.

Import, export and trash are in Projects.
Language, theme and launch after sign-in are in Settings.

Unofficial application. Use at your own risk and back up important projects.
